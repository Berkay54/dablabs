// who-is-it-for

Who needs a mobile app?

Not every business needs an app. If customers contact you only occasionally, a good mobile-friendly website is usually enough. An app makes sense when users come back often, when push notifications matter, or when you need phone features like the camera or location.

We discuss this openly in the discovery call. If a good mobile-friendly website makes more sense than an app, we’ll tell you.

  • Businesses offering ordering, booking or loyalty apps to customers
  • Startups testing an idea with a first version (MVP)
  • Internal apps for field teams and staff
  • Companies that want to offer their website as an app too

// frequently-asked

Mobile App Development: frequently asked questions

Flutter or native?

For most projects, building iOS and Android apps from one codebase with Flutter or React Native is faster and more economical. If you need special features tightly tied to the device, we recommend native development.

Who opens the store accounts?

We recommend opening the Apple Developer and Google Play developer accounts in your name, so the app is published under your name. Apple charges an annual fee and Google Play a one-time fee. We help with account setup and publishing.

How long does it take to build an app?

It varies a lot with scope. That’s why we recommend releasing a first version with the core features and leaving the rest for later versions. We share a clear timeline after the discovery call.

Who maintains the app after launch?

We offer maintenance and support after launch for compatibility with new iOS and Android versions, bug fixes and new features.
