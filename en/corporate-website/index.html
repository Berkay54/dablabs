Who is a corporate website for?

A corporate website is your business’s shop window on the internet. Customers find you through a recommendation or a Google search, and their first impression comes from your site. A site that looks dated, breaks on phones or loads slowly can make even a great company look unreliable.

A good corporate site answers three questions quickly: what do you do, why should I trust you, and how do I reach you? We build the design and content around those three questions.

  • Manufacturers and industrial companies (product catalogue, quote requests)
  • Logistics, construction and service companies
  • Clinics, offices and consultancies
  • New businesses getting their first website
  • Companies that want to redesign an existing site

// frequently-asked

Corporate Website: frequently asked questions

How many pages does a corporate website have?

For most companies, a home page, about page, services or products, references and a contact page are a good start. Giving each service or product group its own page helps you appear in more Google searches.

Can I update the site myself later?

Yes. If your content changes often, we add an admin panel so you can add news, products or references without any coding. If you don’t want a panel, we handle updates for you.

How long does it take?

It depends on the scope, but a simple corporate site with ready content is usually completed in a few weeks. We share a clear timeline after the discovery call.

Can we work together remotely?

Yes. We hold meetings over video calls and share the design and development process online, so location isn’t a barrier.
