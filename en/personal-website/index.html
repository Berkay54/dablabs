Who is a personal website for?

Social media accounts are valuable, but they aren’t yours: algorithms change, accounts get closed and your profile gets lost among other posts. Your personal site is under your control and gives the most accurate information to anyone looking for you.

When a client, employer or patient looks you up after a recommendation, a well-organised site builds trust and makes it easy for them to get in touch.

  • Freelancers, designers and developers
  • Doctors, psychologists, dietitians and lawyers
  • Artists, photographers and musicians
  • Academics, trainers and consultants
  • Professionals who want to stand out in a job search

// frequently-asked

Personal Website: frequently asked questions

I have social media. Why do I need a personal website?

Social media helps you reach new people; a personal site gives anyone researching you an organised, trustworthy address that belongs to you. They complement each other: you add your site link to your social profiles.

Will I appear on Google when someone searches my name?

Searches for your name and profession are usually low-competition, and a well-built personal site can rank near the top for them. Still, we don’t guarantee Google rankings.

Can I add blog posts myself?

Yes. If you want a blog, we add an easy-to-use admin panel. No coding knowledge needed.

Can visitors book appointments online?

Yes. We can set up a system where visitors choose a suitable date and time and send a booking request. Requests arrive in your inbox.
