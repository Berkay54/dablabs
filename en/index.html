Frequently Asked Questions

    

      
How long does it take to build a website?
It depends on the number of pages, the scope of the design and integrations. Simple corporate sites are usually completed in a few weeks; e-commerce and mobile app projects take longer depending on scope. We share a clear timeline after the discovery call.

      
How are website prices determined?
Every project is different, so instead of a fixed price list we quote according to your needs. After discussing the scope, we send a clear written quote.

      
Are sites delivered SEO-ready?
Yes. Technical SEO fundamentals such as semantic HTML, meta tags, sitemap, structured data, fast loading and mobile friendliness are part of our development process. Since SEO also takes content and time, we don’t guarantee search rankings.

      
Can you redesign my existing website?
Yes. We review your current site for design, speed, mobile experience and SEO, then redesign it. Contact us for a free site review.

      
Do you build mobile apps for iOS and Android?
Depending on your needs, we build mobile apps for iOS, Android or both platforms, and support you through the store release process.

      
Do you provide support after launch?
Yes. After launch we offer updates, maintenance and technical support options.

      
Can we work together remotely?
Yes. We work remotely: we hold meetings over video calls and share the design and development process online, so location isn’t a barrier.
