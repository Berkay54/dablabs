// who-is-it-for

Who needs their own online store?

Marketplaces are a good place to start selling, but they take a commission on every sale and the customer belongs to the marketplace, not to you. With your own site, you control pricing, campaigns and the customer relationship.

Your own store doesn’t have to replace marketplaces. Many businesses use both: they find new customers on marketplaces and bring repeat purchases to their own site.

  • Sellers moving from Instagram to their own store
  • Marketplace sellers who want to reduce commissions
  • Producers selling directly to consumers
  • Physical shops moving online
  • Companies selling to dealers at special prices

What determines the price?

The price of an online store depends on its size and the integrations it needs. After discussing the scope we send a written quote. The main factors are:

  • Number of products and categories, and who uploads them
  • Number of payment and shipping integrations
  • Marketplace or accounting software integrations
  • Dealer (B2B) pricing and membership structure
  • Ready-made platform (WooCommerce) or custom software
// frequently-asked

E-Commerce Website: frequently asked questions

Which platform do you use?

It depends on your needs. For a quick start and easy management we use WooCommerce; for stores with special business rules or high performance needs we build custom software with React and Next.js.

Who applies for the payment provider account?

The application is made in your company’s name, because payments go directly to your account. Once it’s approved, we connect the payment system to your site.

Who uploads the products?

You can add products yourself from the admin panel. You can also ask us to do the initial upload.

Are the legal pages included?

The site comes with the structure and consent checkboxes for distance sales, returns and privacy pages. Since the legal text is specific to your business, we recommend having it reviewed by you or your legal advisor.
