Does your site need a redesign?

A website can age within a few years: design trends change, phones get bigger and Google gets stricter about speed and mobile experience. If visitors leave within seconds, or you’re hard to find on Google even when searched by name, it may be time for a redesign.

A redesign doesn’t always mean a brand-new site. Sometimes speed and technical SEO fixes are enough. After the free review we tell you honestly which one you need.

  • Sites that look broken or load slowly on phones
  • Sites that don’t appear for the searches they should
  • Sites that haven’t been updated in years and look dated
  • Sites that are hard to update or whose original developer is unknown
  • Sites that need new services or products added

// frequently-asked

Website Redesign and SEO: frequently asked questions

Will I lose my Google rankings if I redesign?

Not with a properly executed redesign. We permanently (301) redirect old addresses to new ones and keep or improve titles and content. After the switch we monitor for issues in Search Console.

How long does SEO take to show results?

The effect of technical fixes usually starts to show within a few weeks. Reaching the top for competitive searches takes regular content and time, often several months. That’s why we don’t guarantee rankings.

What do you check in the free site review?

We look at how your site appears on phones and desktops, loading speed, indexing status on Google, titles and descriptions, broken links and overall usability.

Can you work on a site another company built?

Yes. If you can give us access to your site’s admin panel or server, we can improve speed and technical SEO on the existing site. If the platform is very outdated, we recommend rebuilding.
